    How to play

    Basics

    1. Each turn a card is revealed from the deck. Treasure is split evenly between everyone still in the mine, straight into their backpacks. The remainder stays on the card.
    2. Everyone still in the mine secretly plays a card: Stay or Leave. The cards flip over together.
    3. Players who leave split the silver left on the cards, then move their backpack into their chest, where hazards can't reach it.
    4. When a second copy of the same hazard appears (on any path), it strikes: everyone still in the mine loses their backpack and is out for this expedition, unless a charm or Guide Dog saves them. One copy of that hazard leaves the deck for good.
    5. Relics (charms, idols and gear) stay on their card. Only a player who leaves alone takes them, along with every other relic on that path. Idols are worth their value at the end. A charm lets you survive its hazard once: when it strikes, spend the charm to keep your backpack and keep exploring.
    6. After the last expedition, the highest score wins: chest + idol values + 5 for each unused charm or gear card. Silver left in a fountain pool doesn't count.

    Special cards

    1. Gear gives you new cards to play: Stash (bank your backpack and stay), Flee (leave ahead of everyone who plays Leave), Guide Dog (escape the next hazard strike with your backpack), Seconds (go back in after you've left), Backstab (leave and rob everyone on your path who plays Leave) and Crystal Ball (any time: privately see the next 3 cards).
    2. Watch out for Mirrors (copy the next card on their path, hazards too: a Mirror followed by a hazard strikes at once), Gremlins (take a share of every treasure), Greedy Urns (grow every turn) and Fountains (double your fountain pool, and let you move silver between it and your backpack).
    3. A Fork splits the mine: after the next decision, every card on that path except the Fork and hazards is discarded, along with any silver on it, and two new paths open. Play Go Left or Go Right. You only share treasure with players on your path, but hazards still count across every path.
    4. Altar takes the backpack of everyone on its path (leavers can collect it), Blessing doubles backpacks, Death ends the expedition for everyone inside (no saving it), and Robbery makes whoever has the most silver in their chest give up a relic. Death and Blessing have a black square on their back.
    5. Gems are auctioned when revealed: everyone gets one bid from their chest, in turn, and only the winner pays. After the expedition a Red Gem lets you secretly add 3 of 5 offered cards to the deck, and a Blue Gem puts a mark of your choice on 3 random deck cards (you see which). Gems won in the last expedition do nothing.
    6. Between expeditions, everyone secretly marks the back of one of three deck cards shown only to them, with one of three emoji. Marks stay for the whole game and show on the top card of the deck. Only you know what your marks mean. The deck is reshuffled every expedition.
