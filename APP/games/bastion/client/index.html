    How to play

    Build a city together, then beat every enemy that comes for it. You start with a keep, a few roads, a lumbermill and a workshop, and a few peaceful rounds to grow before the first enemy arrives.

    Everyone plays at the same time from their own hand. Blueprints place buildings (turn them any way); roads and walls aren't cards: buy them one tile at a time (road: 1 gold, wall: 1 stone; removing one costs 1 gold); each building cares about what's next to it (forest, mountains, water, roads, empty fields, other buildings), gaining or losing output. Work cards make a building produce (Villagers work anything, but without the bonuses); goods reach the shared stockpile at once next to the keep or a storage yard, or a Haul sends a carriage with everything a building holds along the roads, a few cells each round (the card rides along and comes back when it arrives; carriages on the road can be looted). Turn tools into gold with Sell, and buy new cards in the market with the team's gold (dealt anew each round, or reroll it).

    Enemies are cards above the board, each with its own attacks (raids, plague, curses in your deck, blight on the board…) and its own way to be beaten: soldiers, cures, bonfires, razing its lair, paying it off. Beat them all to win; lose if the keep falls. Up to 4 players, or solo.

      How to play

      Goal: beat every enemy before the keep 🏰 falls. The first enemy arrives after a few peaceful rounds, then another every few rounds (the status bar says when). Enemies left 6 rounds after the last one arrives wear the city down: that's a loss too.

      Rounds: everyone plays at once. Each round you draw a fresh hand of 5. Click a card to use it; when you're done, click End turn (you can take it back until everyone is done). At the end of the round the keep makes 1 gold, carriages move and every enemy makes its next attack (▶ on its card).

      Building: click a blueprint, then the board. Turn it with R or the mouse wheel; right-click or Esc cancels. The ghost shows whether it fits and what it would make there. Buildings can go over roads (the road under them is gone; build a new one around). Mines and quarries need a mountain next to them, lumbermills a forest. The blueprint then leaves your deck and the building's crew card joins it.

      Roads and walls aren't cards: click Road or Wall next to your hand, then click empty grass to lay one tile (road: 1 gold, wall: 1 stone; keep clicking to lay more; right-click or Esc stops). Remove takes a road or wall tile away for 1 gold (not a road a carriage is on).

      Working: a work card makes one building produce, once per round. Crew cards (Lumberjacks, Miners…) get the full output; Villagers work any building but only make its base output (penalties still count). Hover a building to see its output and every bonus: what it likes or hates next to it. Enemy blight next to a building costs it 1.

      Goods: buildings next to the keep or a 📦 storage yard deliver straight to the stockpile. Others keep their goods (the badge with the good's icon, e.g. 🪵3) until you play a haul card on them: a carriage 🛒 loads everything there and, at the end of each round, moves along a road towards the keep or a storage yard (Haul: 2 cells a round, Wagon Train: 4). The card rides along, out of your deck, and returns to your discard pile when the goods arrive; the tip on the building says how many rounds that takes. Villagers can haul too (1 cell a round): select them, then click Haul instead. Raiders can loot a carriage on a road (red 🛒 badge) unless the road is next to a wall or near a watchtower. Workshops, smithies and the like take their inputs from the stockpile, so they need a link too.

      Market: 6 cards, dealt anew every round. Reroll deals them anew now for 2 gold, +1 for each reroll earlier in the round.

      Gold: the keep makes 1 a round. Sell turns tools (from a workshop) into gold; Tax gives gold now but puts an Unrest curse in your deck. Building a ⛪ chapel gives you Absolution: click it, click up to 2 other cards in your hand, then confirm to remove them from your deck for good.

      Defence: raids ruin building tiles 🔥 (a damaged building can't work until Masons repair it). Tiles next to a wall, or within 2 of a watchtower 🗼, are safe; when everything is safe, raids break walls instead. Barracks train soldiers 🗡️; spend them on an enemy's card, or click an enemy tile on the board to raze it.

      Curse cards that enemies slip into your deck clog your hand; play one to pay its cost and remove it for good.
