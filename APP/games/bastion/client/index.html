    How to play

    Each player builds their own city with their own resources, and you help each other by trading. Up to 4 players, or solo. There are no enemies (for now): the game goes on until you all vote to end it.

    Setup: in turn, each player places their keep, a house and a farm. The keep gives you 1 unit, a house 2. Every round has three phases that everyone plays at the same time:

    1. Place units on tiles within 3 of your buildings and roads (one unit per tile): on a planned building to build it (each unit removes a build token), on a built one to work it (units stay there round after round), on forest or mountain to gather 1 wood / stone, or on empty grass to build a road or scavenge. 2. Resolve: automatic work happens at once; then you answer prompts one by one ("Spend 3 wood to make 1 tool?"). 3. Plan: with 3 royal decrees from your keep, play cards: blueprints plan new buildings, Tax, Orders and Trade; buy cards from the market with gold.

    Food: at the end of each round your keep makes 1 food and 1 gold, and every unit eats 1 food. Units you can't feed are lost.

    Deck

    How to play

    Your city: every building is one tile and belongs to whoever placed it; you have your own resources (💰 gold, 🪵 wood, 🪨 stone, ⛓️ metal, 🍞 food, 🔨 tools), units 🧍, soldiers ⚔️, deck and hand. The players panel shows everyone's resources; Deck shows a player's cards. There are no enemies yet: the game goes on until everyone votes to end it.

    Reach: your units and plans only go within 3 tiles (diagonals count) of your built buildings and your roads. One unit per tile, whoever owns it.

    1. Place (everyone at once). Click an outlined tile to put a unit there; click it again (or right-click) to take it back. Hover a tile: the details panel says what a unit would do there.

    • Your planned building (greyed out, 🔨n): each unit removes one build token and comes straight back. At 0 it's built (a house adds 2 units).
    • Your built building: farms (2 food), lumber mills (3 wood) and quarries (3 stone) produce on their own; a mine asks for 1 metal or 3 stone; a workshop asks whether to turn 3 wood into 1 tool. A barracks turns the unit into a soldier for good. A house does nothing yet. Units stay on buildings every round (only Orders sends one back), so sometimes you'll have nothing left to place.
    • Soldiers go on towers (they don't do anything yet) and return to your soldier pool every round.
    • Forest / mountain: gather 1 wood / 1 stone. Empty grass: build a road (it extends your reach) or scavenge (a die: nothing, 1 wood or 1 stone). These units come straight back.

    Click Done placing when you're finished (Not done takes it back until the last player is done).

    2. Resolve. Everything automatic happens at once (see the chronicle). Then the prompt panel asks about each of your units that has a choice, one by one; the tile is highlighted on the board.

    3. Plan. Your keep gives you 3 royal decrees 📜 every planning phase; every card costs 1 decree (plus what else it says). Click a card to play it:

    • Blueprints (bought in the market): click an outlined tile to plan the building there. It appears greyed out with its build tokens; units build it in later rounds. The blueprint leaves your deck.
    • Tax: +1 gold, but an Unrest card goes into your discard pile (pay 1 decree and 1 food to get rid of it). Orders: click one of your buildings with a unit on it: the unit goes back to your pool for the next round. Trade: give another player 3 different resources or 2 of the same one.
    • Market (below the board): blueprints on the left, actions on the right, bought with your gold into your discard pile. It stays the same between rounds; each half can be reshuffled once per round for 1 gold.

    End of round: soldiers return to their pool, your keep makes 1 food and 1 gold, and every unit and soldier eats 1 food. Units you can't feed are lost. Then hands are discarded and you draw 5 new cards.
