    How to play

    Grow a city together, keep its people fed, and beat every enemy that comes for it. You start with a keep, a house, a farm, a lumbermill and a few roads, and some peaceful rounds to grow before the first enemy arrives. Up to 4 players, or solo.

    People are your workers. Houses and the keep hold the city's population; each round the fed people become units, shared out among the players. First everyone places their units at the same time: one unit per building that makes something, any number on Hauling, on Fighting an enemy, or on an enemy tile to raze it. Then, one player at a time, you act: resolve your units (each works its building, hauls goods or strikes an enemy once), play cards from your hand, buy in the market and lay road and wall tiles, then end your turn.

    Buildings come from blueprint cards (turn them any way) and care about what's next to them: forests, mountains, water, roads, other buildings. Goods from a building next to the keep or a storage yard go straight to the stockpile; others wait in the building until a hauling unit brings them in (3 goods, 5 with a Carriage). A tool from the stockpile gives a worker +1. Roads and walls are bought in threes; removing one is free.

    Food: at the end of each round the city eats (bread feeds 4, wheat feeds 2). Hungry people don't work next round. Enemies are cards above the board with their own attacks and their own way to be beaten. Beat them all to win; lose if the keep falls.

    How to play

    Goal: beat every enemy before the keep 🏰 falls. The first enemy arrives after a few peaceful rounds, then another every few rounds (the status bar says when). Enemies left 6 rounds after the last one arrives wear the city down: that's a loss too.

    Units: houses 🏠 (2 people each) and the keep (2) are the city's population. At the start of each round the people fed at the last upkeep become units, shared equally among the players (the rest go one each from the first player ★, who moves one seat on every round).

    1. Place (everyone at once). Click an outlined building to put a unit there (one unit per building per round, first come; buildings belong to nobody), click Hauling above the board, the Fighting pool on an enemy card, or an enemy tile on the board. Hover first to see what it would do. Click your unit again (or Take back on a pool) to remove it. Shift+click a pool to put all your free units in it (and, on your turn, Shift+click a Fighting pool to make all your fighters there strike). Click Ready when you're done; Not ready takes it back until the last player is ready. Everyone's units show as tokens in their colour.

    2. Act (one at a time, in turn order). On your turn your units are outlined: click one to make it act, once, in any order. Then play cards, buy in the market, lay tiles, and click End turn: units you didn't resolve are lost. Cards, the market and tiles only work on your turn.

    • Work: the building makes its output (the green badge): its base plus bonuses for what's next to it, -1 per enemy blight next to it. With tools 🔨 in the stockpile you can spend 1 for +1 output (the inspector offers both; Shift+click works with a tool). Inputs (wood for a workshop...) come from the stockpile, so the building needs a link: next to the keep or a 📦 storage yard, or a road reaching one.
    • Goods: a building next to the keep or a storage yard delivers straight to the stockpile. Others keep their goods (badge like 🪵3, up to 6) until hauled. Gold, soldiers, keep repairs and cures never wait.
    • Haul: a unit on Hauling moves up to 3 goods from one building (linked by road) to the stockpile at once, biggest piles first. Each 🛒 Carriage makes one haul a round carry 5. With several buildings to haul from, you pick one (the default is the one with the most goods).
    • Fight: a unit on an enemy is 1 strike; every "per damage" strikes deal 1 damage, and the rest carry over (shown on its card). A unit on an enemy tile is 1 strike toward razing it.

    3. End of round: the keep makes 2 gold and 1 wheat, then the city eats: each bread 🍞 feeds 4 people, each wheat 🌾 feeds 2 (the least food that covers everyone). People left unfed are hungry and don't become units next round; the status bar shows what the next upkeep will eat. Then every enemy makes its next attack (▶ on its card). Farms grow wheat (more in clusters), bakeries turn wheat into bread.

    Building: click a blueprint, then the board. Turn it with R or the mouse wheel; right-click or Esc cancels. The ghost shows whether it fits and what it would make there. Buildings can go over roads (the road under them is gone). The blueprint then leaves your deck.

    Roads and walls aren't cards: click Road or Wall next to your hand, then click empty grass to lay one tile from the team's stock (status bar). With the stock empty, the next tile buys 3 (roads: 1 gold, walls: 1 stone). Remove takes a road or wall away for free (no refund).

    Market: 6 cards, dealt anew every round, bought with team gold. Blueprints come in bundles (e.g. House ×3: three copies join your discard pile). Reroll deals them anew for 2 gold, +1 for each reroll earlier in the round.

    Gold: the keep makes 2 a round. Sell turns tools into gold; Tax gives gold now but puts an Unrest curse in your deck. Building a ⛪ chapel gives you Absolution: click it, click up to 2 other cards in your hand, then confirm to remove them from your deck for good.

    Defence: raids ruin building tiles 🔥 (a damaged building can't be worked until Masons repair it) and loot goods waiting in buildings. Tiles next to a wall, or near a watchtower 🗼, are safe; when everything is safe, raids break walls instead. Barracks train soldiers 🗡️: spend them with Strike on an enemy's card, or click an enemy tile on your turn to raze it.

    Curse cards that enemies slip into your deck clog your hand; play one to pay its cost and remove it for good.
