    How to play

    Work together to hold the keep through 10 sieges. Each level gives you a few build turns: everyone plays at the same time, spending labor (3 per turn) to play cards from their own hand — towers, walls, traps, barracks, farms and orders. Play a structure card on the same structure to upgrade it. When everyone has ended the turn, the next one starts, and after the last one the enemy marches down the road. You always see which enemies come next.

    Enemies that reach the keep damage it; at 0 the realm is lost. After each siege held, everyone earns gold and visits the market to add cards to their own deck. Up to 4 players (or solo); enemies grow tougher with more defenders.

      How to play

      Goal: hold the keep 🏰 through all 10 levels together. Enemies walk the road to the keep; each one that arrives takes its damage off the keep's health. At 0 the game is lost.

      Build turns (everyone at once): each turn you discard your hand, draw 5 cards and get 3 labor ⚒ (+ your quarries) and gold 💰 from your farms. Click a card, then a highlighted cell. Walls, spike pits and tar pits go on the road; everything else on grass; barracks next to the road. Orders take effect when clicked (Master Mason: click it, then a structure). Play a structure card on the same structure to upgrade it (costs +1 labor per level it already has; it's also fully repaired). Right-click or Esc cancels. When you're done, click End turn (you can take it back until everyone is done).

      Siege: after the last build turn the enemy attacks. Towers fire at the enemy nearest the keep; walls and soldiers block ground enemies, who stop to smash them; flyers 🦇 ignore walls, soldiers and traps, and only towers that hit flyers can shoot them. Armor reduces every hit (ballistas, wizards and spikes ignore it). Forges ⚒️ add damage and watchtowers 🗼 add range to the towers next to them; a farm next to barracks makes soldiers return twice as fast. Damaged walls stay damaged; destroyed walls are gone.

      Market (after each siege held): everyone gets the level's gold. Buy as many cards as you can afford — they go into your own deck — and retire one card from your deck for 1 gold. New cards unlock as the levels go on. Click Done when finished.

      Tips: stop enemies with a wall right where several towers reach; boiling oil 🔥 beside a wall is brutal; farms early pay for the market later; watch the next siege's enemies and roads, the flank road opens at level 6.
