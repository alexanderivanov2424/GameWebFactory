    How to play

    Everyone plans at the same time, in secret. Place structures inside your territory, train units, order moves and attacks, research advances and trade, then press End turn. When all players are done (or the timer runs out) every order resolves together.

    Every structure works as long as it has its workers (people, who eat food and need housing). Gold, faith and science go straight to your realm, and goods (food, wood, stone, metal) into your stores (Town Halls, Storage Yards, Granaries). Production and upkeep happen together; only what's left at the end of the turn must fit in your stores (the rest is lost). You grow when you make more food than you eat and have free housing. Structures take time to build: each needs a few turns of work, and every turn of work needs its builders, people without a job (oldest site first). Switch structures off (or all at once with Jobs off) or demolish them to free people for building. You can build anywhere in your territory; to build further out, Extend territory (Build tab): gold claims an explored cell next to your land, with its free neighbours, at the end of the turn. Structures don't spread your land: your first city gives you free land (3 cells around it) and other Town Halls hold only the cells they stand on. This land is strong: first come, first served, it stays yours while the Town Hall stands, and cells two realms reach in the same turn go to nobody. Land bought with gold is weak (dashed border) and costs 1 gold a turn per cell (if you can't pay, you give up the cells you can't pay for, farthest from your Town Halls first): it becomes nobody's when an enemy unit stands on or next to it (you can't claim there either), when it's cut off from your strong land, or when another realm's Town Hall reaches it. Nothing can be built where an enemy unit stands, and a construction site with an enemy unit on it gets no work. Units keep a standing order across turns until you give them another: Explore (clear the fog), Fight (attack the nearest enemy unit in sight) or Pillage (attack the nearest enemy structure in sight, fighting enemy units that come close first); click a cell beyond a unit's reach to walk there over several turns. Your own units pass through each other. Units with orders are greyed out. Units fight with dice wherever they meet: each die that rolls 4 or more hits (5 or more against a unit standing in forest, hills or mountains, or on its own structure). Lose every Town Hall and you are out. Units appear on the structure that trains them. A military structure can recruit every turn on its own (when you can pay and have people without a job), and give each unit it trains a standing order. Click a cell again to cycle through what's on it (a unit, then the structure under it). Click a resource on the top bar for its resource view: the map turns grey and shows what makes it (green, +n a turn) and what uses it (red, −n). Tech tree (next to the Build tab) shows the science and faith advances. When a turn resolves, a replay visits each fight you saw with the dice (you on the left); Skip or Esc stops it, and Settings → Hex Realms sets its speed or turns it off.

    The last realm standing wins; if several survive to the turn limit, the highest score wins. The host can add bots (computer players) in the settings; once no person is left in the game, it ends and the highest score wins.
