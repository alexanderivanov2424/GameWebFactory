    How to play

    Everyone has an empty 9×9 board, a deck of shape cards and a hand of 3. On your turn, play as many cards as you like: place the card's shape on your board (rotate with R, right-click or the mouse wheel; shapes are never mirrored), or discard a card to place a single blank tile (Patch). Changed your mind? Undo (U or Ctrl+Z) takes back your last placements until a row or column clears or your turn ends.

    A full row or column clears. Each ● coin on a cleared cell gives 1 gold; each ⚔ attack deals 1 damage to the opponent you're aiming at (change your target any time); each 🛡 shield gives 1 shield, which absorbs incoming damage point for point and is used up (it also drops by 1 at the start of each of your turns); each ✝ chapel gives a card removal: spend it any time with Delete under a hand card to remove that card from your deck for good.

    👺 Goblin cards (purple, from the market) are placed on an opponent's board instead of yours. A cleared row or column with a purple goblin tile in it has no effect (the 👺 on it is just decoration), except on grey tiles (like siege cards), which goblins can't spoil.

    ⚙ Siege: every 3 siege symbols you clear give 💪 1 strength, which adds 1 to the total damage of each of your attacks (2 attack symbols then do 3 damage). Cards marked 🗑 (goblin and siege cards) leave your deck once played.

    The market (the host picks which, its size and the card packs in it: base, chapel, goblin and siege cards): buy one card a turn with gold; bought cards go to your discard pile. You start with 1 gold if you go first, 2 if you go second, and so on.

    • Conveyor Market: a row of cards (5 by default) priced by position: slot 1 costs 1 gold, slot 2 costs 2, and so on. A bought slot stays empty until the turn ends; then the cards shift towards slot 1 and new ones fill the end.
    • Cost Market: face-up cards (10 by default), each with its own cost (roughly its number of tiles, +1 with an attack, +2 or +3 for small cards that are mostly symbols). Buy any one, or instead remove one from the market (it leaves the game; that's your buy for the turn). Empty slots refill when the turn ends.

    Ending your turn draws you back up to 3 cards. At 0 health you're out; the last one standing wins.
