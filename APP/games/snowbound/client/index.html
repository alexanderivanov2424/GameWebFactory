    How to play

    • A huge valley is snowed in. Click snow next to cleared ground to shovel it: every unit of snow pays you $1.
    • Buy machines that drive out and clear snow on their own, and upgrades that make everything faster. Snow gets deeper further from the cabin.
    • Each player has their own money and machines; the valley, the stove and the woodpile are shared.
    • Stay alive: keep the shared stove burning logs (or you get frostbite), keep rations (or you starve) and diesel for your machines.
    • Blizzards come with a 20 second warning: no deliveries, logs burn fast and snow piles back up.
    • Catch golden snowflakes for a frenzy or a cash bonus. Clear the goal share of the valley to win.
    Click: shovel · Drag or right-drag: pan · Wheel: zoom · Shift+click: send your machines there
