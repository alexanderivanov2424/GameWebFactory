    How to play

    • Everyone builds roads in the same city. Houses and shops pop up in colours; cars drive from houses to shops of the same colour.
    • Drag to draw roads (diagonals too); right-drag to erase. Each player has their own pieces, and you can only erase your own.
    • Drag straight into water or a mountain to build a bridge or tunnel. Motorways, roundabouts and traffic lights are tools on the bottom bar.
    • Every road tile you build earns you 1 point for each car that drives over it (either way), so busy tiles pay most.
    • Every week everyone gets 20 roads and picks one of the same two upgrades (the city pauses while you pick).
    • Shops pile up pins when nobody serves them. If a shop's timer fills up, everyone loses.
