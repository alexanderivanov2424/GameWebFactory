    How to play

    Everyone works together. All 40 cards are dealt: red, blue, yellow and green 1-9 and black trumps 1-4. Whoever holds the black 4 is the captain and leads the first trick. Follow the suit led if you can; the highest black wins a trick, otherwise the highest card of the suit led. The winner leads next.

    Each mission's tasks are drafted first, starting with the captain. Complete them to fuel and supply the ship; fail them and the ship may take damage. Each mission costs 1 fuel and 1 food. The run ends when the hull is gone or every mission deck is empty.
