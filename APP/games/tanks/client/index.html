    Game settings

    Each tank hides on a secret square and lays 2 secret mines. Scan: more than half the rows or columns 1 turn, a single one 3 turns, otherwise 2. Spray: 1 damage to a 2×2 area. Artillery: 2 damage to any one square. Blast (5 turns): 4 damage to the first tank in a straight line from you; a miss reveals nothing. Move 1, heal 1 HP 3, build a wall 1, remove a wall 2, lay a mine within 3 steps 1. Mines deal 1 damage to everyone on the square when someone moves onto it. A tank that takes damage is shielded until its next turn. Walls block moving, blasts and mine range. The board always stays connected, so 100% walls makes a maze.
