    Game settings

    Run a colony together. Colonists dig, haul, build and look after themselves; you give the orders (dig, build, deconstruct, priority) and everyone sees everyone's cursor and orders live. Any player can pause or change the speed. Keep your colonists breathing, fed and clean. Hotkeys: Space pause, 1/2/3 speed, G dig, C cancel, X deconstruct, P priority, I inspect, Esc/right-click cancel.

    How to play
    • Dig out rock with Dig (G): drag a rectangle. Colonists mine it and leave debris.
    • Build Ladders up from a floor so colonists can climb; put a Storage Bin down so debris gets hauled.
    • Oxygen: build an Algae Oxygenator, powered by a Manual Generator joined to it with Wire.
    • Food: a Nutrient Press (dirt + piped water) or Fungus Beds.
    • Toilet: an Outhouse; add a Wash Basin to fight germs.
    • Anyone can pause (Space) or change speed (1/2/3). Everyone sees everyone's plans in their colour.
    • Hotkeys: Space pause, 1/2/3 speed, G dig, C cancel, X deconstruct, P priority, I inspect, Esc/right-click cancel.
