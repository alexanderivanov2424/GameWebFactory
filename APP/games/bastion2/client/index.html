    How to play

    Grow your own city with a deck of cards. Up to 4 players, or solo. No enemies yet: the game goes on until you all vote to end it.

    Setup: place your Castle, then a House and a Farm next to it.

    Each turn you hold 5 new cards (plus any you kept). Play resource cards into your play area to fill this turn's counters (🪵 wood, 🪨 stone, 🌾 wheat). Each Hut turns 1 wheat a turn into 🍞 food, which you keep (up to 5). Buy a structure from the market with your action (1 per turn): pay its resources and 🧑 population and place it on a tile next to your buildings. It's built at the end of your turn.

    Villagers must be fed: pay 1 food or discard 2 cards. Discard one unfed and you get Hunger, then Starvation, which costs you population.

    How to play

    Map: drag to pan, mouse wheel to zoom, C to centre on your castle. Hover a building to see what it does.

    Hand: click a card to play it. Resource cards go to your play area and fill the counters at its top right, under your permanent food and population (temporary: cleared at the end of your turn). Each Hut's free action turns 1 🌾 wheat into 1 🍞 food, which you keep (up to your food storage). Upkeep cards (Villager, Hunger, Starvation) ask how to pay. The 📌 on a card keeps it in your hand when your turn ends.

    Building: click Buy on a structure in the market (right) and the tiles next to your buildings light up: click one to place it there. You pay its resources, population and 1 action at once; it's built at the end of your turn. Until then, right-click it (or Take back in the play area) to undo and get everything back.

    Population (🧑 available / total) is what your builds take this turn; it comes back every turn. Actions (the big circle): buying a card costs 1, the King costs 1, some structure actions cost 1 and some are free. Each structure's action works once per turn (the list on the left).

    End turn: your planned builds are built, your play area and every unkept card are discarded, and you draw 5. Discarding an unpaid Villager adds Hunger to your deck; an unpaid Hunger adds Starvation; an unpaid Starvation costs 1 population and a Villager.
