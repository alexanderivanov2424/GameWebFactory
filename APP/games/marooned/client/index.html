    How to play

    You're shipwrecked. Explore the island, gather food and materials, build a shelter and then a raft, and be at camp when it sails. One of you is secretly the Imposter, who scores when the others don't get away. Everyone scores on their own: exploring, building, escaping, relics, staying fed, guessing the Imposter in the nightly votes, and secret objectives.

    Each day you spend your energy (5, +1 after a night in a shelter, less when hungry, wounded or sick). Gathering rolls a die only you see; you may announce a worse roll than you got, never a better one. When a camp stage is full, the builder picks a helper, draws 3 check cards, secretly discards 1 and passes 2; the helper reveals 1. Each night everyone votes on who they suspect (only the tally is shown), gets hungrier, and sleeps in the shelter if there's room. Deal with suspects yourselves: refuse to help them, or attack them.
