    How to play

    One player, picked at random, is Interpol. Everyone else is a smuggler with two ships.

    • Smugglers: each ship gets one action per turn: move up to its speed (ships can stop on the dots along a route), or pick up cargo at a planet with a crate token: draw 5 goods, load any of your hand onto the ship face down, declare which legal good it carries, and discard down to 5.
    • Fly the ship to the contract's destination and deliver (free): your largest set of one legal good pays its value per card, +the contract's bonus if it's the good the contract asks for, and every illegal good pays its own big value.
    • Interpol plays a small deck-building game: play cards (1 action per turn; Funding is free), buy one new card with the budget, draw 3. Barrier, Checkpoint and Patrol cards each give a piece on the board: barriers block routes; a loaded ship on the same spot as a checkpoint or patrol is stopped and inspected, so patrols chase ships down.
    • An inspection lets the smuggler offer a bribe first; then Interpol takes it, or searches the ship: anything that isn't the declared good is seized, with a fine.
    • When every contract is delivered, the richest smuggler wins, unless Interpol's score (seized goods, fines and bribes) is higher.
