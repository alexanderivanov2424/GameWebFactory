    How to play

    • Race from the start spaces to the golden city at the far end of the map. With 2 players, each has two explorers and both must arrive.
    • Select a card in your hand, then click a highlighted space next to your explorer. Jungle needs machetes (green), water needs paddles (blue), villages need coins (yellow); pips show how many. Jokers (grey) count as any one of them.
    • One card carries one explorer over several spaces of its kind until its value runs out. Switching cards loses what's left; cards are never added together for one space.
    • Rubble (grey): discard that many cards. Base camp (red): remove that many cards from the game. Mountains are impassable, and no two explorers share a space.
    • Blockades sit on tile borders. Pay one like a space to take it: the border opens for everyone (your explorer stays put). Blockades break ties.
    • Buy one card per turn. Coin cards and jokers pay their value, every other card pays ½. Reserve cards can only be bought once a market slot has sold out.
    • Action cards (purple) are played for their effect. Single-use cards leave the game once used.
    • End your turn: played cards and any cards you pick are discarded, then draw back up to 4. Unplayed cards may stay in hand.
    • When someone's explorers have all arrived, the round finishes. Most blockades wins a tie, then the highest blockade, then who arrived first.
